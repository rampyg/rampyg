## Track 3: LinkedIn, three times a week

LinkedIn punishes daily, so run three posts a week: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, mid-morning. Use the LinkedIn sections of the series files.

You have begun the Robotics series on LinkedIn, so continue from there:
- Finish Robotics (anchor plus 21) at three a week: about seven weeks.
- Then Disconnected Edge (anchor plus 8): about three weeks.
- Slot the AI Agents LinkedIn series in wherever you prefer, before Robotics as the foundation, or after as a look back.

LinkedIn runs slower than X on purpose. The two tracks do not need to line up. X is the daily engine; LinkedIn is the steady, higher-effort channel.
