# PAD Coverage Map

Triage of all 124 published Prior Art Disclosures (PADs) against the social content already written (the AI-agent series and the robotics series). Purpose: decide which PADs still need their own article or post, and skip the ones our posts already explain.

Legend: **Covered** = a published post already explains this PAD's idea. **Partial** = a post touches it, but the PAD has a distinct angle worth its own piece. **Uncovered** = no post explains it.

Post codes: AI-C1..C7 = AI-agent concepts (1 Identity, 2 Verifiable Credentials, 3 JCS+Data Integrity, 4 Multikey+PQ, 5 Delegation, 6 Continuous trust, 7 Sidecar). R1..R21 = robotics concepts (see robotics series file).
